Facts:
- Henry Sy Sr. (Chinese immigrant from Jinjiang, Fujian) opened a small shoe store called ShoeMart (SM) on Carriedo Street, Quiapo, Manila in 1958.
- Expanded from shoes into clothes, accessories, and full department store goods.

- SM City North EDSA opened in 1985, pioneering "malling" as an air-conditioned family destination (shopping, dining, cinema, leisure).
- Later expanded to SM Megamall (1991), SM Mall of Asia (2006), plus banking (BDO), hotels, and real estate (SM Prime / SMDC).
