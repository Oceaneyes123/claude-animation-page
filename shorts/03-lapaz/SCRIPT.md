# "Ang Disyertong Gawa ng Ilog at Hangin" — La Paz Sand Dunes, Ilocos Norte
Format: cartoon-3d-short (vertical 1080x1920 -> 1440x2560, 30 fps, flat bean-head characters in painted 3D world).
Facts:
- The La Paz Sand Dunes span roughly 85 square kilometers along the coast of Laoag City, Ilocos Norte (part of the broader Laoag–Paoay coastal dune system) and are declared a National Geological Monument. (Wikipedia / Geological studies)
- Unlike inland deserts (such as the Sahara) formed by lack of rain, La Paz is a coastal sand dune system beside the West Philippine Sea in a tropical, typhoon- and monsoon-swept region. (Geomorphology / Film Philippines)
- The nearby Padsan River carries eroded mineral particles and sand down from the Cordillera mountains to the coast, while ocean waves and coastal currents push alluvial and marine sediments back onto the shore. (ResearchGate / Geomorphology accounts)
- Strong seasonal monsoon winds (Amihan and Habagat) pick up and transport the dried coastal sand inland via aeolian saltation and suspension, sculpting rolling ridges and parabolic dunes partly anchored by hardy coastal grasses and vines. (ResearchGate / Geomorphology accounts)
Everyday Analogy:
- Pouring dry sand or grains in front of a strong electric fan: even in a wet room, a steady supply of grains blown by constant airflow piles up into miniature hills in the corner.

Voiceover (`video3/narration.wav`):
Bakit nga ba may malawak na disyerto sa gitna ng maulan at laging binabagyong Ilocos Norte?

Ganito kasi yan. Sa baybayin ng Laoag, may walumpu't limang kilometro kuwadradong buhanginang parang piraso ng Sahara—ang La Paz Sand Dunes.

Sa unang tingin, nakakalito: paano magkakaroon ng disyerto sa isang lugar na binabagsakan ng malalakas na bagyo at ulan taon-taon?

Nagsisimula ang lahat sa Padsan River, na humahakot ng tone-toneladang pinong buhangin mula sa kabundukan pababa sa dagat. Pagkatapos, tinutulak naman ito ng mga alon pabalik sa pampang.

Suwerte—sagana sa ilog at dagat ang Ilocos para sa mga bukirin at mangingisda. Malas—kapag natuyo ang buhangin sa pampang, sinasalubong ito ng walang-tigil na hanging Amihan at Habagat na bumubuga papasok sa lupa.

Parang pagpatak ng tuyong buhangin sa tapat ng malakas na electric fan: basta tuloy-tuloy ang suplay ng butil, tinatangay ito ng hangin at itinutumpok bilang mga higanteng burol.

Hanggang ngayon, buhay at gumagalaw ang La Paz Sand Dunes—isang disyertong hindi ginawa ng tagtuyot, kundi ng tubig at hangin. Kaya ang tanong...
